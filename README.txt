KSTU ICT HELP DESK - MERGED STUDENT GUIDE PROJECT

How to open:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the folder named "Sammy Pro".
3. Double-click "index.html".
4. The student will see five choices:
   1. How to log in to your portal
   2. How to reset your student portal
   3. How to log in to your virtual classroom
   4. How to reset your virtual classroom password
   5. How to connect to KsTU Wi-Fi

The original guide pages and screenshots are retained.
Each guide also has a "Back to Student Guides" button.
