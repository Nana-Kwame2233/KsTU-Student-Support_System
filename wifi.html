HOW TO CONNECT TO KSTU-WIFI
  1. Your KSTU email and new password is used to connect to the WIFI. The password sent to you in the SMS is temporary and cannot connect to the KSTU-WIFI.
  2. For those with Iphone or Windows devices:
    • Turn on your wifi when you are on KSTU campus and you will see KSTU-WIFI
    • Click on it, enter your username and password to join or connect
    • When you are presented with a security warning click continue or trust to get connected
  3. For those with android device type with do not validate:

      4. Open KSTU_WIFI

    • Enter your fill: username = identity,
    • Enter thepassword,
    • CA certificate = don’t validate,
    • EAP method = PEAP,
    • Phase 2 authentication = MSCHAPV2

      Click connect and continue to trust the security warning to be connected

    • Some android phones do not have the “do not validate” or do not verify under the CA certificate drop down. In these phones you will only see install certificate

  4. Follow these steps in such cases:
    • Download the CA certificate from http://tinyurl.com/2bmxp3n5 with the phone
    • Open the wifi and under the CA certificate drop down select install certificate
    • Browse through the phone and pick the downloaded file, name it and install it
    • Now reopen the wifi and make the settings like the one in this screen shot
    • The name given to the certificate here is KsTU Cert, with your email at identity without the @kstu.edu.gh, domain (kstu.edu.gh) and password click connect
Tips:
  • Ensure you enter your registered email correctly
  • Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the reset email
  • If issues persist, contact KsTU IT Support at info@kstu.edu.gh
