HOW TO RESET YOUR STUDENT PORTAL PASSWORD
  1. Go to the "KsTU Student Portal"
    • Enter student.kstu.edu.gh
  2. Click on “Forgot Details?”
  3. Choose “Recover Details”
    • Enter your index number
    • Enter your phone number used in school
    • Click on Initiate Reset
  4. Enter the code number
    • A code will be sent to your registered phone number
    • Enter the code and click Send
  5. You will receive a new password
  6. Return to the portal and log in with your student number and the new password
Tips:
  • Ensure you enter your registered email correctly.
  • Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the reset email.
  • If issues persist, contact KsTU IT Support at info@kstu.edu.gh.
