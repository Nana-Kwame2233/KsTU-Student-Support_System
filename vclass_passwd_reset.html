HOW TO RESET YOUR VIRTUAL CLASS PASSWORD
  1. Go to the"KsTU Virtual Classroom"
    • Enter vclass.kstu.edu.gh
    • Enter your Email account the school gave you
    • And Click Enter
  2. Click on “Forgot my password”
  3. To Recover your account “Begin with”
    • Entering your Email that the school gave you
    • Enter the characters from the images that appears or Listen to the audio
    • Click on Next
  4. Get back into your account “Why are you having trouble signing in?”
    • Select from this two options:
        • - I forgot my password
        - I know my password but still can't sign in
    • Click on Next
  5. Please choose the contact “For text you will receive a text message with a verification code:” or " For call you will receive a phone call with a verification code:”
    • Text my mobile phone:
        • - Enter your phone number
        - Click on Text
    • Call my mobile phone
        • - Enter your phone number
        - Click on Call
  6. Enter “the verification code you received to your text message” or Enter "the verification code you received from your phone call"
    • Text my mobile phone:
        • - Enter your verification code
        - Click on Next
    • Call my mobile phone
        • - Enter your verification code
        - Click on Next
  7. “Choose a new password”
    • Enter new password
    • Confirm new password
    • Click on Finish
Tips:
  • Ensure you enter your registered email correctly.
  • Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the reset email.
  • If issues persist, contact KsTU IT Support at info@kstu.edu.gh.
