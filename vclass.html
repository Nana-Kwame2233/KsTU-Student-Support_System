HOW TO LOGIN TO THE VIRTUAL CLASSROOM
  1. Go to the"KsTU Virtual Classroom"
    • Enter vclass.kstu.edu.gh
    • Enter the KSTU email the school gave you
  2. Enter “Your Password”
    • Click on Sign in
  3. "Verify your identity"
    • Click on Text +XXX XXXXXXXX42
  4. Enter “Enter code”
    • Click on Verify
  5. “successful open to your Virtual Class page”
Tips:
  • Ensure you enter your registered email correctly.
  • Check your spam or junk folder if you do not see the reset email.
  • If issues persist, contact KsTU IT Support at info@kstu.edu.gh.
